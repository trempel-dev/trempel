# Fruity Spin

A skin of the slot template: a 1920×1080 landscape scene with generated art. `slot.svg` — the base of the
standard slot heir (the slot contract's ids; `#reels` is a 3×3 field of 230×200 cells), `popups/` — the
free-spin and big win popups, `ui/` — its own prefabs (reel tile, spin button, foliage), `art/` and
`symbols/` — the art, `symbols.json` — feed letters → symbols. Panels, buttons and icons come from the
kit's default skin (`@skin/…`, the template's `.trempel/project.mdz`).
